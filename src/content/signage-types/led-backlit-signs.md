  - q: "What is the difference between front-lit and halo-lit letters?"
    a: "Front-lit letters shine light forward through a translucent face. Halo-lit letters have a solid face and shine light backwards onto the wall, which makes a glow around each letter."
  - q: "Which is easier to read from far away?"
    a: "Front-lit letters are usually easier to read from a distance, because the whole face of each letter is bright. Halo-lit letters give a softer look that suits closer viewing."
