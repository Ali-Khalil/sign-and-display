The best file to send for a signboard is a vector file of your logo, such as a CorelDRAW, Illustrator, SVG or PDF file, with the text converted to outlines and your colours named. If you only have a picture, send the largest and clearest one you have and it can be redrawn.

## Why does a signboard need a vector file?

A signboard is much larger than a business card, and its letters are cut by machine along exact outlines. A vector file stores shapes as lines and curves, so it stays sharp at any size and gives the cutter a clean path. A photo or screenshot is made of dots, which turn blurry and jagged when enlarged.

## What should I send?

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| The logo as a vector file | Enlarges cleanly and can be cut accurately. |
| Text converted to outlines (curves) | The design opens correctly even if the font is not installed. |
| Colour references | Brand colour codes let the nearest acrylic or vinyl colour be matched. |
| The exact wording and spelling | Typed, not only inside an image, for names, phone numbers and Urdu text. |
| A photo of the wall or shopfront | Shows the space, the background colour and anything in the way. |

## What if my logo has very fine details?

Thin lines, tiny text and small gaps that look fine in print can be too delicate to cut as 3D letters or to bend in neon. They are usually thickened, simplified or printed flat instead. This is shown in the design you receive, so you can approve the change before anything is made.

## What if I do not have a logo at all?

Send the name and describe what you like: colours, a style of lettering, or photos of signs you have seen. A design is prepared for you to approve. You can also try ideas yourself in the [sign designer](/design-your-sign/) and send the result.

## How should I send everything?

Send the files and details on WhatsApp. If a file is too large or gets compressed, send it as a document rather than as a photo, so it arrives unchanged.
