The price of a 3D sign board in Pakistan depends mainly on six things: the overall size, the number and height of the letters, the material, whether the letters are lit, whether there is a backing board, and how hard the sign is to fit. Prices change with material costs, so this guide explains what moves the price rather than quoting figures.

## What decides the price of a 3D sign board?

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Letter height and count | Each letter is cut and built separately, so more and taller letters mean more material and more work. |
| Material | Acrylic, metal-finish and mirror finishes cost different amounts per sheet. |
| Lighting | Lit letters need LED modules, wiring and a power supply, and must be built hollow. |
| Backing board | Letters on an ACP or glitter board add the cost of the board and its frame. |
| Design complexity | Logos, fine scripts and many small parts take longer to cut and fix. |
| Fitting | Height, access and distance to the site affect installation. |

## Do lit letters cost more than non-lit letters?

Yes. A lit sign carries everything a non-lit sign has, plus the lighting. Front-lit letters need a translucent face and LEDs inside each letter. Halo-lit letters need LEDs facing the wall and stand-off fixings. Both need a power supply and a tidy cable route to a power point.

## Does a bigger sign always cost more?

Mostly, but not in a straight line. Doubling the letter height roughly quadruples the face area, and taller letters are usually built deeper as well. A slightly smaller letter height can cut the price noticeably while still being easy to read. The [guide to sign sizes](/guides/what-size-should-my-shop-signboard-be/) explains how to choose a height from the viewing distance.

## How can I keep the cost down?

- Use the shortest form of the name that customers recognise.
- Choose the letter height from the real viewing distance, not the largest that fits.
- Light only the main name and leave the second line non-lit.
- Fix letters straight to a clean wall if you do not need a backing board.

## How do I get an exact price?

Send the name, the approximate width of the space, the type of sign you have in mind and your city on WhatsApp. A photo of the wall or shopfront helps. You can also design the sign yourself in the [sign designer](/design-your-sign/) and send that. You receive a design and a quote to approve before anything is made.
