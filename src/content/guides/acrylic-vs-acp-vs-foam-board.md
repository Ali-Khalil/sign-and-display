Acrylic, ACP and foam board are the three materials most signboards are made from. Acrylic is used for raised letters and lit faces, ACP for flat outdoor boards, and foam board for low-cost indoor signs. The right choice depends on whether the sign is indoors or outdoors, lit or non-lit, and how long it must last.

## What is each material?

- **Acrylic** is a hard, glossy plastic sheet. It comes clear, coloured, mirror-finished and in translucent white for lit signs. It cuts cleanly into letters and logos.
- **ACP** (aluminium composite panel) is two thin aluminium skins bonded to a plastic core. It is flat, rigid and light for its size.
- **Foam board** is a lightweight PVC foam sheet. It is easy to cut and print on, and the cheapest of the three.

## How do they compare?

| | Acrylic | ACP | Foam board |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical use | 3D letters, lit faces, nameplates | Shopfront boards, backing for letters | Indoor signs, displays, events |
| Outdoors | Yes | Yes | No |
| Can be lit from inside | Yes (translucent sheet) | No | No |
| Look | Glossy, solid, premium | Flat, clean, many finishes | Flat, matt |
| Relative cost | Higher | Medium | Lower |

## Which should I choose for a shopfront?

For most shops the answer is a combination: an ACP board across the shopfront as the background, with raised acrylic letters for the name. The board gives a clean, weatherproof surface and the letters give depth. If the wall itself is in good condition, acrylic letters can be fixed straight to it with no board.

## Which should I choose for an office or reception?

Acrylic. Raised acrylic letters or a logo on the wall behind the reception desk look solid at close range, and can be halo-lit for a soft glow. Foam board is fine for room labels, notices and anything that will be replaced often.

## When is foam board the right choice?

When the sign is indoors and the budget or the time is short: event backdrops, exhibition displays, menus, seasonal offers and temporary signs. It is light enough to fix with tape and cheap enough to replace.
