Front-lit, backlit and halo-lit signs all use LEDs, but the light leaves the sign in different directions. Front-lit letters glow through their face, backlit signs light a whole translucent panel from behind, and halo-lit letters stay solid while light spills onto the wall around them.

## What is a front-lit sign?

A front-lit sign has LEDs inside each letter and a translucent acrylic face. The letter itself is the bright part, and its sides stay dark. It is the most visible style from a distance.

## What is a backlit sign?

A backlit sign has a translucent panel with lights behind it, as in a light box. The whole face glows, and the design is printed or applied on it. It suits full-colour designs, logos with many colours and long text.

## What is a halo-lit sign?

A halo-lit sign has solid letters mounted a little way off the wall. The LEDs face backwards, so each letter is outlined by a glow on the wall. The letters read as dark shapes against the light.

## How do the three compare?

| | Front-lit | Backlit | Halo-lit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bright part | Letter face | Whole panel | Wall behind the letters |
| Read from far away | Best | Good | Fair |
| Look | Bold, commercial | Even, colourful | Soft, premium |
| Wall needed | Any | Any | Plain, matt, light-coloured |
| Typical use | Shopfronts | Light boxes | Receptions, boutiques, offices |

## Which should I choose?

- Choose **front-lit** if the sign must be read from across a road at night.
- Choose **backlit** if you need a full-colour design or a lot of text to glow.
- Choose **halo-lit** if the sign is seen from nearby and should look refined rather than loud.
