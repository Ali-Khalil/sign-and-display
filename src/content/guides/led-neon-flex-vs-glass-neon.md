LED neon flex is a flexible strip of LEDs inside a coloured tube, while glass neon is a glass tube filled with gas that glows at high voltage. LED neon is lighter, safer, cheaper to run and harder to break, which is why most new neon signs for shops, cafés and homes are now made with it.

## How does each one work?

**Glass neon** is made by heating and bending glass tubes by hand, filling them with gas and powering them with a high-voltage transformer. The gas itself glows.

**LED neon flex** is a row of small LEDs sealed inside a flexible tube that spreads the light into a continuous line. It runs at low voltage from an adapter and is bent to shape and fixed to an acrylic backing.

## How do they compare?

| | LED neon flex | Glass neon |
|---|---|---|
| Voltage | Low | High |
| Heat | Stays cool | Gets warm |
| Breakage | Flexible tube, hard to break | Fragile glass |
| Power use | Low | Higher |
| Weight | Light | Heavier |
| Fitting | Fixed to an acrylic backing | Needs careful mounting and wiring |
| Look | Even line of light | Classic all-round glow |

## When is LED neon the better choice?

For almost every new sign: shop windows, café walls, salons, wedding and event décor, and rooms at home. It can be hung like a picture, moved to a new place and switched on from a normal socket through its adapter.

## When might someone still choose glass neon?

When the authentic look matters more than anything else, for example restoring an old sign or matching a vintage theme. It costs more to make and to run, and needs more care.

## What should I decide before ordering a neon sign?

- The words or logo, and whether they are in English, Urdu or both.
- The colour of the light.
- The overall width, which sets how small the letters can be.
- The backing: clear or coloured acrylic, as a rectangle or cut to shape.
