Front-lit, backlit and halo-lit signs all use LEDs, but the light leaves the sign in different directions. Front-lit letters glow through their face, backlit signs light a whole translucent panel from behind, and halo-lit letters stay dark while light spills onto the wall around them.

## What is a front-lit sign?

A front-lit sign has LEDs inside each letter and a translucent face. The letter itself is the bright part.

## What is a backlit sign?

A backlit sign has a translucent panel with lights behind it, as in a light box. The whole face glows and the design is printed or applied on it.

## What is a halo-lit sign?

A halo-lit sign has solid letters mounted slightly off the wall. The LEDs face backwards, so each letter is outlined by a glow.
